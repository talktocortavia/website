About

Helping businesses think beyond traditional AI.

Cortavia is an online AI solutions company. We are a team of AI experts, data scientists and business consultants who want AI to do useful, practical work.

Who we are

Fully online, close to your problem.

We work entirely online, partnering with clients remotely to understand their challenges, find the opportunities, and build AI that fits the way they already work.

Our vision

A future where AI is part of every part of business operations, making organisations more agile, more data-driven and more centred on their customers.

Our mission

Deliver production-ready AI that solves real business problems, technically strong and aligned with each client’s goals.

Our approach

What you can expect from us.

Start with the work, not the model
We look at the process first. Sometimes the right answer is a simple workflow and no AI at all, and we will say so.
People stay in charge of people decisions
Anything that affects someone’s job, grade or pay is a recommendation for a person to review, never an automatic outcome.
Only the data that is needed
We ask for the minimum, write down where it goes, and agree with you how it is stored and for how long.
Honest reporting
You will hear what worked and what did not, including the parts of a pilot we would not repeat.
