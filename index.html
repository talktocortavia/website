How we work

Small steps, shown early, handed over properly.

  1. 1

    Listen

    We start with how the work actually happens today: the tools, the hand-offs, and where time goes.

  2. 2

    Scope

    We pick one or two places where AI genuinely helps, and say plainly where it would not.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build with your documents and systems, and show working versions early so you can steer.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    You get something your team can run, with documentation and a way to check how it is performing.

How we think

We would rather tell you AI is not the answer than sell you one that is.

Start with the work, not the model
We look at the process first. Sometimes the right answer is a simple workflow and no AI at all, and we will say so.
People stay in charge of people decisions
Anything that affects someone’s job, grade or pay is a recommendation for a person to review, never an automatic outcome.
Only the data that is needed
We ask for the minimum, write down where it goes, and agree with you how it is stored and for how long.
Honest reporting
You will hear what worked and what did not, including the parts of a pilot we would not repeat.
